======================================================================
1. WHAT THE SITE IS
======================================================================
SadServers is a hands-on Linux, DevOps and SRE practice and assessment platform ("Like LeetCode for Linux and DevOps"). Users get a live, disposable cloud VM with a real broken system and must fix it (or complete a task), then click "Check My Solution". Two audiences:
  - Individuals: learning, practice, certificates/achievements.
  - Businesses: candidate interviews, internal training, AI agent evaluation.
Company: SadServers Inc., federally incorporated in Canada, based in Waterloo, Ontario. Created 2022. Bootstrapped and profitable, no outside funding. Supported by The Accelerator Centre.

6. OBSERVATIONS ON THE SITE'S DESIGN
======================================================================
  - Consistent global header/footer; each page has a unique <title> and an eyebrow label in caps for marketing pages.
  - Marketing pages (Individuals, Business) use card grids, numbered steps, and a closing CTA.
  - Functional pages (Scenarios, Ranking) are table based.
  - Several pages share the generic home <title> (Scenarios, Gift, Redeem, Sign Up), which is weak for SEO.
  - The Scenarios page shows a "new interface (beta)" for registered users, so signed-in users may see a different layout.
